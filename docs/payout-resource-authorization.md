# Private resource evidence and native payout expense reservation

`authorizePayoutDispatch` consumes trusted server-side observer/simulation evidence for a saved payout. It is NOT a public endpoint or a live observer; no customer-provided balances, energy figures, approval flags or nonce may be passed here. This service does not prove RPC authenticity or provider delivery.

Before issuing dispatch approval it binds exact TXID/network/sender and immutable quote/operation, validates the saved signature/net transfer, requires SQL-clock evidence freshness (observation no more than 30 seconds old, validity no longer than 30 seconds from observation), enough USDT and enough native balance for the full signed fee limit. BSC additionally requires pending nonce to equal the signed nonce, positive simulation estimate within signed gas, and no resource rental cost. TRON requires an active account, positive trusted energy/bandwidth requirements and observed delivered amounts sufficient for both. Conservative native balance is required even with rented energy; do not assume fee_limit includes every possible bandwidth/activation expense.

The per-network policy starts DISABLED with zero caps. Only an operator-reviewed private policy may enable a per-payout native-unit cap and UTC daily native-unit cap. Under its row lock, usage includes ALL unresolved HELD claims from any date plus SETTLED claims settled today. Native cost reserved is signed maximum fee + attributed resource-provider cost. The reservation and exact signature-bound dispatch approval commit together; any failure rolls both back. Exact evidence replay does not reserve twice; changed evidence conflicts instead of refreshing/resigning automatically. Expired approval/unknown execution never releases a budget claim.

Successful payout confirmation marks an existing reservation SETTLED in the same transaction as its journal. This retains the conservative maximum, not an invented measured expense/refund. Failed-chain finality, actual fee measurement, expense adjustments, provider refunds and abandoned-request recovery remain unimplemented. Resources may already have been purchased before observation; this post-delivery gate does NOT authorize or cap the earlier purchase. A separate pre-purchase shared budget claim/resource-order outbox is still required. Limits here are payout-only, not the combined sweep+withdrawal cycle or a unified treasury budget.

These are TRX-sun / BNB-wei caps, not USD customer fees and not the agreed $3 TRON cycle estimate. No live native-to-USD valuation or $3 enforcement is implied. No reserve auto-refill occurs.

The migration is prepared only, RLS enabled, normal-app grants revoked. No policy was remotely enabled or changed. No live chain/resource observation, provider order, purchase, API credential use or broadcast was performed. Dedicated reviewed runtime privileges and real PostgreSQL contention tests remain required.

Fixtures cover disabled/per-payout/daily policies, insufficient token balance, mismatched nonce, stale evidence, previous-day unresolved budget carryover, persistence failure rollback, no duplicate claim, changed-evidence conflict, final success settlement and missing TRON activation/energy/bandwidth. All chain/provider observations are simulated.
